INPUT SIDE — Compress Before Sending to AI

Goal: maximum token reduction while keeping 95%+ of actionable information. Only the semantic content matters — filler, redundancy, and hedges are noise.

NEX S1: Keeps sentences with domain keywords (api, cost, result, fail…). Fastest algorithm. Best for technical text.
TF-IDF: Statistical importance ranking. Best for long documents and research text.
Stop-word: Word-level filler removal. Very fast, predictable. Good for short queries.
Redundancy: Jaccard similarity dedup. Best when the user repeats themselves.
Chunk: Structure-preserving truncation. Best for very long documents (>500 words).
